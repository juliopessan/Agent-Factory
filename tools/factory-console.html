Factory Console

Offline view and configuration helper for factory.json and run-ledger reports. It never executes commands, sends data, or approves work; Invoke-FactoryManifest.ps1 -Action Validate and Factory CI remain authoritative.

Load factory.json

Choose the file from the Factory root or paste its contents. Data stays in this page; nothing is uploaded.
